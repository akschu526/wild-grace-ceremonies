The Philosophy

Sacred and wild were never opposites.

I believe a wedding ceremony should feel like the truest possible version of your love story — reverent where you want reverence, a little magic where you want magic. I hold space for both.

The Sacred

Raised Catholic and comfortable weaving scripture, tradition, and rite into your ceremony — for couples who want their day to feel rooted in faith and blessing.

The Wild

Handfastings, moon phases, botanical blessings, and yes — a line from your favorite fantasy or sci-fi saga read aloud like scripture of its own. Whimsical, never watered down.

Meet Your Officiant

How We Work Together

Three ways to bring Wild Grace to your day

01

The Custom Ceremony

A fully bespoke script built from our conversations — your story, your readings, your traditions (sacred, whimsical, or both). Includes a planning call, a written draft, and one revision round.

02

The Guided Elopement

Intimate, low-fuss, still meaningful. A shorter ceremony designed for small gatherings, courthouse steps, or a clearing in the woods — legally binding, beautifully spoken.

03

Vow Renewal & Handfasting

For couples renewing their promises or adding a ritual element like handfasting or a unity rite to an existing ceremony — religious, secular, or somewhere in between.
